Editorial Policy

Yohoho 77 aims to publish useful, original, and clearly written browser game pages. Our editorial goal is to help visitors understand what each game offers before they play.

Original Descriptions

Game descriptions and guides are written for Yohoho 77 and should not be thin copies of other sites. We focus on gameplay style, controls, categories, user experience, and practical tips.

Updates and Corrections

We may update game pages when titles change, links break, descriptions need improvement, or policy information becomes outdated. Visitors can report issues through the contact page.

Advertising Separation

Advertising helps support the website, but ads should not decide editorial wording or game descriptions.
