    "We Become What We Behold" is not just a game; it's an interactive experience that invites players to delve into the depths of their own mind and explore the profound impact of perception on their lives. Developed by the independent studio "The Looking Glass", the game encourages introspection and self-discovery, leaving players with a powerful sense of reflection and personal growth.

    The premise is simple yet profound: players are presented with a series of evocative images, each representing a different aspect of their life, be it their relationships, ambitions, or fears. As they gaze upon these images, a voice, soft and introspective, guides them through a series of questions and prompts, inviting them to confront their own internal narratives and examine the stories they tell themselves about the world and their place within it.

    "We Become What We Behold" is not a game of quick victories or high scores. It is a deliberate and thoughtful journey into the depths of self-awareness. The gameplay, if you can call it that, revolves around the interplay between the player's gaze and the voice's guidance. Each image acts as a catalyst for contemplation, prompting players to:

Players are not forced to confront their inner demons, but rather guided to do so at their own pace, creating a space for introspection and self-acceptance. The game's gentle approach allows for a sense of safety and vulnerability, making it a powerful tool for personal growth and development.
